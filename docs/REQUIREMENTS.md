# REQUIREMENTS.md — traceability matrix against the assignment brief

> Generated from `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md.tmpl`; every number resolves from `results/metrics.json` (which composes
> `results/parts/*.json`). One row per numbered requirement in sections 5-18 of the brief (every sub-bullet of
> 6.1, 14 and 15 included), plus the two section-2 behaviours the brief singles out. Written as a reviewer aid:
> the point is to confirm coverage in two minutes and to see where the evidence is thin.

**Status rule.** **MET** = the requirement is satisfied and an artifact in the *Evidenced* column shows it.
**PARTIAL** = addressed, but the measured evidence shows a shortfall, a stated target was missed, or the
mechanism's benefit is not demonstrated. **NOT DONE** = no artifact. No row is MET on an assertion; where the
evidence column would be empty the row would be NOT DONE (there are none). Documents: `T` =
`docs/TECHNICAL.md`, `R` = `docs/RESULTS.md`, `D` = `docs/DATA_CARD.md`. All data is synthetic; read the numbers as
properties of this simulator (`T` section 0).

## The three strongest and the three weakest results

**Strongest.**

1. **The ranker beats popularity and content cosine on unbiased labels.** NDCG@10 0.1842 against
   0.0523 (popularity) and 0.1336 (cosine), paired Wilcoxon p=1.3e-14 against
   cosine, above cosine in 5 of 5 seeds (mean gap +0.0623 NDCG@10). (The first tag had concluded the opposite; that conclusion was an artifact of a train/serve skew and is retracted in `T` section 5.1.)
2. **Hard constraints.** 0 violations in 6631 recommended POIs against
   974 in 671 trips with the brief's additive formula (DR1).
3. **Oracle-free retrieval that does not eliminate the long tail.** Candidate recall 0.926 overall /
   0.898 long-tail against chance 0.553 / 0.481; every selection was made on train-carved
   validation and the true-utility oracle only scored results.

**Weakest, each with the measurement behind the framing.**

1. **Long-tail precision 0.196 against the 0.40 target.** The pool's own long-tail positive rate is 0.097, so the
   served list is a 2.03x lift and the raw ranker a 3.54x lift (raw precision
   0.343); the MMR curve runs from 1.90x (lambda 0.5) to 2.70x (lambda 1, no diversity term), so
   diversity costs precision but does not explain the gap to 0.40. The 0.40 was set a priori, without reference to that base rate (`T` section 10.2). The genuine weakness: precision is lost *after*
   ranking (raw 0.343 -> served 0.196) and which scoring step is responsible was not isolated.
2. **Responsiveness to a stated touristiness preference.** Scenario-4 overlap 0.538 against at most 0.35; real-trip flip Jaccard
   0.904 overall, diluted by |pref| near zero (0.973 bottom tercile,
   0.834 top). The simulator encodes the preference (trip-level Spearman
   -0.51), so this is a model limitation: the ranker reproduces
   83% of the outcome gradient for cold-start trips and
   -1% for trips with history. Untested fixes: a preference-consistency term in the utility layer, or a hard filter (`T` section 3.1).
3. **Confidence values are barely informative about ranking quality.** Decile Spearman 0.358 against at least 0.6; trip-level Spearman
   0.064. The earlier 0.879 came from leaked features; reweighting the confidence terms
   on holdout labels would be tuning on the holdout and was declined (`T` section 10.1).

Also MISSED and diagnosed in `R`: % of oracle ceiling 50.0% against 70%, localness index Spearman 0.582 against 0.6, and the
within/cross-archetype ratio 1.20 against 2.0 (unreachable by construction: a perfect ranker gets 1.89).
